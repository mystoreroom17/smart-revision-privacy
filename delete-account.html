4. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained (कौन सा डेटा हटाया जाता है)

Data Permanently Deleted Upon Account Deletion:

  • Firebase Authentication Account: Your login credentials, email record, display name, linked Google/Password provider identifiers, and unique Firebase User ID (uid).
  • Student Profile Document (users/{uid}): Your profile settings, study cycle counters, daily question set state, and user-level subscription status fields.
  • Saved & Custom Revision Cards (users/{uid}/cards): All custom questions, note-generated flashcards, starred bookmarks, and mistake flashcards created by your account.
  • MCQ Practice & Test History (users/{uid}/attempts): All question attempt logs, selected answers, accuracy statistics, and timestamps.
  • Mistake Notebook & Personal Notes (users/{uid}/mistakes): All recorded wrong questions, revision counts, mastery status, and personal study notes.

Shared Application Content & Legal Retention:

  • Shared Question Bank & Public Exam Syllabus (Preserved): The shared bilingual MCQ question bank (synced from the master Google Sheet and built-in starter questions), public exam categories, subject catalogs, and other students' accounts are shared platform resources and are never altered when an individual user deletes their account.
  • Active Google Play Subscriptions: Deleting your app account does not automatically cancel an active recurring billing mandate in the Google Play Store. If you have an active auto-renewing subscription (Plans V1–V5), please also cancel auto-renewal in Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Financial & Tax Compliance Records: Server-verified payment transaction receipts (payments/{paymentId}) are retained only to the extent strictly required for statutory tax, accounting, and anti-fraud audit compliance.
  • Processing Timeframe: In-app account deletion is executed immediately upon re-authentication. Public web deletion requests submitted via this page or email are verified and completed within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days).
