1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized exam practice, daily revision tracking, mistake notebook synchronization, and account management across sessions, the Application collects and stores the following categories of data:

1.1 Account & Profile Information

  • Student Name (Display Name): Provided by you during Email/Password registration or retrieved from your Google profile when you use Google Sign-In.
  • Email Address: Used for account creation, login authentication, password reset emails, and account identification.
  • Authentication Credentials & Identifiers: A unique Firebase Authentication User ID (uid), email verification status, authentication provider type (Email/Password or Google), account registration date (creationTime / createdAt), and last login timestamp (lastSignInTime / lastLoginAt). Passwords entered during Email/Password sign-up are securely hashed and processed directly by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible or stored in plaintext by us.

1.2 Educational Activity & Study Progress Data

When you practice Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) in Hindi or English, the Application stores your study data in Google Cloud Firestore under your unique user path (users/{uid}) and caches it locally on your device while you are signed in:

  • MCQ Practice Attempts (users/{uid}/attempts): Question ID, target exam ID, subject ID, your selected option (A, B, C, or D), official correct option, whether your answer was correct, practice mode (subject practice, daily revision, or mistake revision), question rotation cycle number, and attempt timestamp/date.
  • Mistake Notebook & Personal Revision Notes (users/{uid}/mistakes): Questions answered incorrectly, wrong attempt count, revision count, mastery status, date of last mistake and resolution, repeated-mistake flags, and any custom personal revision notes (personalNote) that you type into the app.
  • Saved & Custom Revision Cards (users/{uid}/cards): Bookmarked/starred questions, custom questions or flashcards created from your notes (bilingual question text, options, correct option, explanations, exam/subject tags, and save timestamps).
  • Study Plan & Daily Quota State (users/{uid}): Active plan identifier (e.g., Free tier daily question limit tracking), subscription status, current question cycle number, and daily question usage counts.

1.3 Device Permissions & Technical Data

  • Android Permissions: The Android application requests only standard network access (android.permission.INTERNET) required to communicate with Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and question bank updates. The Application does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, phone call logs, SMS messages, or GPS location.
  • Local Device Storage: The Application uses WebView/browser localStorage on your device to cache the bilingual MCQ question bank, language preference (Hindi, English, or Bilingual), and active session progress. User-specific local session data is automatically cleared when you tap the Logout button.
  • Network & Security Metadata: When connecting to Firebase services, Google's servers automatically process standard technical information such as your IP address, device user-agent string, and cryptographic authentication tokens to maintain session security and prevent unauthorized access.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for educational and application functionality purposes:

  • To register, authenticate, and maintain your student account across sessions.
  • To send password reset emails through Firebase Authentication when you use the Forgot Password feature.
  • To synchronize your MCQ attempts, scores, accuracy statistics, saved cards, and Mistake Notebook across sessions so your progress is not lost if you refresh or reinstall the app.
  • To calculate your daily question rotation, pending wrong-question revisions, and subject-wise / exam-wise accuracy in your Progress Report.
  • To enforce daily practice limits based on your active plan tier.
  • To allow authorized system administrators to view registered student account status and aggregate or account-level study metrics (such as registration date, last login date, total attempts, correct/incorrect answers, and accuracy percentage) for student support, quality assurance, and platform administration.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical errors, unauthorized access, or abuse.
No Sale of Personal Data & No Third-Party Behavioral Advertising: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, email address, or study history to advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

3. Firebase & Google Authentication

Smart Revision – All Exams uses Google Firebase Authentication to provide secure sign-up and login options:

  • Email and Password Authentication: When you sign up with an email address and password, Firebase Authentication securely hashes your password and issues a short-lived, cryptographically signed JSON Web Token (ID token) to verify your identity.
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, the Application receives your basic Google account profile information (your name, email address, and email verification status) via Google OAuth 2.0 solely to create and authenticate your student profile. We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail messages, Contacts, or Calendar.
  • Forgot Password: When you request a password reset, Firebase Authentication sends an automated password reset link directly to your registered email address.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account and learning data are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication infrastructure managed by Google LLC. We implement the following technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Strict UID-Based Data Isolation: Every student's study records are stored under their authenticated Firebase UID (users/{uid}/cards, users/{uid}/attempts, and users/{uid}/mistakes). Server-side Firebase Security Rules strictly enforce that a regular student account can read and write only their own data and can never read, list, or modify another student's profile, email, or progress.
  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All communications between the Android/Web application and Firebase/backend servers are encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS. Data stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Restricted Administrative Access: Administrative endpoints and dashboards require cryptographic verification of the caller's Firebase ID token via the Firebase Admin SDK and are restricted exclusively to authorized administrator accounts.
  • Logout Session Cleanup: When you log out of the Application, your local session data (attempts, mistakes, and starred card caches in localStorage) is cleared from the device.

While we implement strict security controls and server-side access rules, please note that no method of electronic transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% immune to all potential risks.
